# SubControl — SaaS MVP Prototype

- **Audience:** Consumers and small businesses
- **Value Proposition:** Audit your business subscriptions and automate cancellations from one dashboard.
- **Competitor Gap:** Competitors only offer monitoring, not execution.
- **Target Pricing:** Subscription fee of $49 per month per business.
- **Autonomy Boundary:** Interactive web prototype + waitlist capture only. No billing or automated outreach is active.
