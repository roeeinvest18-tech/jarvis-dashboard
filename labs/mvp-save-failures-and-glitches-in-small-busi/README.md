# LedgerGuard — SaaS MVP Prototype

- **Audience:** Small business owners, freelancers, and bookkeepers using mobile apps for financial management.
- **Value Proposition:** Real-time error tracking and data integrity diagnostics for small business accounting
- **Competitor Gap:** Major software ignores small mobile bug reports, allowing niche solutions to charge a premium.
- **Target Pricing:** Usage-based SaaS at $39/month for small businesses.
- **Autonomy Boundary:** Interactive web prototype + waitlist capture only. No billing or automated outreach is active.
